## Security and operational acceptance

Server-side roles, TLS website, private uploads, audit history, search/filter and mobile layouts have implementations. Payment security, email and live database acceptance still need the external activations above. Encrypted full backups and isolated restore tooling are available in `scripts/ngo-backup.mjs`; automatic offsite scheduling is an operational setup item, not a completed disaster-recovery claim. Notifications refresh every 30 seconds while the relevant dashboard is visible.
